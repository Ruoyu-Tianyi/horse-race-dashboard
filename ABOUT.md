# About · Team 11 · RACE EDGE — Hong Kong Neon

[English README](README.md) | [中文 README](README.zh-CN.md)

## English

The Hong Kong Neon edition of RACE EDGE reimagines the same historical racing analysis as a red-and-gold, poster-led prediction dashboard. Traditional sign lettering, an AI-generated adult equestrian portrait, condensed display numerals and a three-runner podium create a distinctive interface while preserving the model, market comparison and independent validation.

- **Branch:** `design/hong-kong-neon`
- **Team:** Team 11 — Zhou Tianyi, Xu Yiming, Zhang Zai and Xu Mohan.
- **Data:** Hong Kong race records from 2014–2017; prediction replay covers the independent 2017 holdout.
- **Scope:** three headline metrics, two analytical charts, race filters, runner histories, CSV export and a research watchlist.
- **Evidence:** 509 held-out races; model top-pick hit rate 25.1%, historical market favourite 29.1%. These results do not establish market outperformance or profitable betting.
- **Stack:** Python 3.14, Streamlit, Pandas, Plotly and scikit-learn.

## 简体中文

RACE EDGE 的港式霓虹版本：以大红大金和海报式首页重新呈现同一套历史赛马分析。传统招牌文字、AI 生成的成年女骑师形象、粗窄数字字体与横排金榜三甲构成鲜明视觉，同时保留原有模型、赔率比较和独立检验。金色柱表示模型概率，粉色菱形表示市场基准。图片和字体均随项目保存，运行时不需要图像 API。设计与素材记录见 [assets/DESIGN.md](assets/DESIGN.md)。

- **团队：** Team 11 — Zhou Tianyi、Xu Yiming、Zhang Zai、Xu Mohan。
- **数据：** 2014–2017 年香港赛马记录，预测回放只覆盖 2017 年独立检验期。
- **功能：** 三个核心指标、两张分析图、赛事筛选、马匹近况、CSV 导出与研究观察清单。
- **验证：** 509 场独立检验赛事；模型首选命中率 25.1%，历史赔率热门马 29.1%，不能据此声称跑赢市场或实现盈利。
