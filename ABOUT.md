## English

The classic analytical edition of RACE EDGE: a Streamlit dashboard for Hong Kong horse-racing history, form-based win probabilities, market-odds comparisons and chronological model validation. Its dark interface uses lime probability bars and amber market markers to keep race-by-race research compact and readable.

- **Branch:** `main`
- **Team:** Team 11 — Zhou Tianyi, Xu Yiming, Zhang Zai and Xu Mohan.
- **Data:** Hong Kong race records from 2014–2017; prediction replay covers the independent 2017 holdout.
- **Scope:** three headline metrics, two analytical charts, race filters, runner histories, CSV export and a research watchlist.
- **Evidence:** 509 held-out races; model top-pick hit rate 25.1%, historical market favourite 29.1%. These results do not establish market outperformance or profitable betting.
- **Stack:** Python 3.14, Streamlit, Pandas, Plotly and scikit-learn.

## 简体中文

RACE EDGE 的经典分析版本：使用 Streamlit 整理香港赛马历史、展示基于历史表现的胜率、比较市场赔率，并按时间验证模型。深色界面以青柠色概率柱和琥珀色市场标记突出逐场研究，强调清晰、紧凑的信息呈现。

- **团队：** Team 11 — Zhou Tianyi、Xu Yiming、Zhang Zai、Xu Mohan。
- **数据：** 2014–2017 年香港赛马记录，预测回放只覆盖 2017 年独立检验期。
- **功能：** 三个核心指标、两张分析图、赛事筛选、马匹近况、CSV 导出与研究观察清单。
- **验证：** 509 场独立检验赛事；模型首选命中率 25.1%，历史赔率热门马 29.1%，不能据此声称跑赢市场或实现盈利。
